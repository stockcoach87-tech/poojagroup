PUBLISHING ON YOUR DOMAIN
1. Log in to your domain hosting control panel (for example, cPanel).
2. Open File Manager and enter the website document root, usually public_html.
3. Upload the contents of this folder (not the enclosing folder) into public_html.
4. Make sure index.html is directly inside public_html.
5. Enable an SSL certificate for your domain and force HTTPS through your hosting panel.
6. Open the website on a mobile phone and a desktop browser. Test phone and email links and the enquiry form.
